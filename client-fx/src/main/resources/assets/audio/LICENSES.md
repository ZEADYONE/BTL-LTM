# Audio asset licensing

All audio files under this directory are original procedural outputs generated
specifically for Bomberman Online Mini by
`client-fx/audio-tools/generate_audio.py`.

- Third-party samples or recordings: **none**
- External music compositions: **none**
- Generator random seeds: fixed and repository-controlled
- Runtime assets covered: every `.wav` and `.mp3` below this directory

The generated audio assets are made available under the **CC0 1.0 Universal**
public-domain dedication. A copy of the legal text is available at:
<https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode>

The generator source follows the license of the repository's source code. The
CC0 dedication applies to its rendered audio outputs, not to third-party
software used to encode them. FFmpeg is a build-time encoder and is not bundled
by this audio pack.
